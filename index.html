Meet the founder

Meet Tasha Jackson

MS, LPC, NCC · Licensed Professional Counselor

I'm Tasha Jackson, a Licensed Professional Counselor and the creator of Tasha the Therapist. I created these therapy-inspired workbooks to make the reflection, insight, and practical tools used in therapy more accessible for everyday life.

My goal is to help you better understand yourself, build healthier patterns, and move forward with meaningful changes—one page at a time.

A little reflection can be the beginning of meaningful change.

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about using the workbook collection.

Are these workbooks a replacement for therapy?

No. These workbooks are self-guided reflection resources, not a substitute for individualized mental health care, diagnosis, or treatment.

Where can I purchase the workbooks?

Use the Shop Workbook links above to visit the current shop. Direct product purchase links will be connected before launch.

Can I work through a workbook at my own pace?

Yes. The exercises are designed for personal reflection, so you can pause and return as needed.
